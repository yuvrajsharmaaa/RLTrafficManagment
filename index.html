🚑 Ambulance Route Dispatch Guide

This system guides an emergency ambulance through dynamic Delhi traffic to the nearest trauma center:

  • ETA to Hospital: Shows remaining drive time. Ticks down to reflect time saved when the route re-plans.
  • Traffic Unpredictability: Tracks road congestion changes. Higher unpredictability triggers automatic route re-planning.
  • Adaptive vs. Baseline: Compares real-time adaptive routing against a fixed standard route.
